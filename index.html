How to use Qarbon

Getting started

  • The home screen shows one world at a time, starting with the black hole. Swipe left or right to move between them (on a computer: the ‹ › buttons or the arrow keys). Review is the Earth, Decks the Big Dipper, Practice the Moon, B.A.R. Exam the Sun (tap it and watch), Focus the Crab Nebula, Media the Qarbon atom wearing headphones, and Reading the Helix Nebula. Tap a world to go straight there. The black hole opens Review on your last deck. The small line under each world shows cards due, your deck, tonight's Moon, or the books you're reading.
  • They're all real: the black hole is Sagittarius A*, the one at the center of our galaxy, as photographed by the Event Horizon Telescope. The Earth is live: it turns, and it's lit where the Sun really is right now, with city lights on the night side. The Moon shows tonight's actual phase, and the stars behind everything are the real night sky (see Space images below).
  • The app opens on the deck you used last (Beginner 150 the first time).
  • Pick what you're doing from the Mode menu at the top: 🃏 Review, 🗂 Decks, 🔁 Practice, 🔥 B.A.R. Exam, ⏱ Focus, 📖 Reading or 🎧 Media.
  • Built-in stacks: Beginner 150 and Level 2 (150 more words). All my cards has everything.
  • Switch decks in 🗂 Decks mode: tap Switch ▾ at the top and pick one. The deck you're in is highlighted.
  • Make your own deck: in Decks → Switch ▾, tap + New deck from a CSV file, pick the file and name it. It stays saved. A word that's in two decks shares one set of progress.
  • Your own decks have ✎ to rename them and 🗑 to delete them. Deleting a deck keeps its cards in All my cards.
  • CSV format: the first row needs front (French) and back (English). Optional columns: part_of_speech, gender (m, f or m/f), example_target, example_english, tags (levels like A1, A2, B1, B2, separated by ;).
  • On the study page, ‹ (top left) or the Qarbon logo goes back to the black hole, ⚙ opens the settings, and ? opens this page.

Review

  • Shows the cards due today, plus a few new ones (set how many under New cards per day).
  • Flip: tap the card, tap Show answer, or press Space/Enter. Tap again to peek back at the front.
  • Rate it: Again, Hard, Good or Easy (keys 1 to 4). The small text under each button is when you'll see the card next.
  • Swipe right for Good, left for Again (after flipping).
  • Again brings the card back a few cards later today, and again tomorrow.
  • Scheduling works like Anki: every card has an "ease" (starts at 2.5). Good multiplies the wait by it. Hard grows the wait a little and lowers the ease. Easy jumps further and raises it. Again resets the card to 1 day. Words you find hard come back more often.
  • 🔊 says the word or the example sentence. The card's frame color shows the word type: noun green, verb pink, adjective orange, adverb purple, pronoun blue.
  • Reverse cards: in ⚙ → Card direction, each stack can be French → English, English → French (you answer in French; the card shows EN → FR), or Both. Each direction has its own schedule. With Both, a new word's English → French side unlocks once you've seen it French-first.

Decks

  • Browse every card in the current deck: search, filter by level or word type, and see each card's status (new, due today, or its next date).
  • Practice these practices just the cards you filtered.
  • Tap a card to edit it. Change the spelling, meaning, word type, gender, example or tags; its progress stays.
  • Suspend a card to stop it coming up in Review, Practice and Burn (it shows as "suspended" in the list). Unsuspend it any time.
  • Delete removes a card and its progress. Words from Beginner 150 and Level 2 can be suspended but not deleted.
  • + Add card makes a new card. In your own deck it goes into that deck; otherwise it goes into All my cards.

Practice

  • Goes through every card in the stack in random order. It never changes your schedule, so practice as much as you like.
  • Cards you miss come back at the end, and you get a score when you finish.

🔥 B.A.R. Exam (Burn After Reading)

  • Choose 🔥 B.A.R. Exam in the Mode menu. It takes 10 cards you've never studied from the stack (fewer if that's all that's left).
  • Read each one once, and hear it. There's no going back.
  • The cards burn up. Then for each English word, pick the exact French spelling out of 4: the right one, an accent trap, a spelling trap, and another real word.
  • Right counts as Good, wrong as Hard, and missed words go first in your next Review. The end screen shows how many "survived the burn".

⏱ Focus (timer and music for any studying)

  • Choose ⏱ Focus in the Mode menu (or ⤢ Focus page in the small timer dropdown). The Mode menu takes you straight back to Review or anything else.
  • At the top is the ♪ music dropdown: closed, it shows what's playing; tap it for the player and your lists. Then a big clock with a ring that empties as time passes. Start/Pause, Reset, and Skip (jump to the break, or back to focus; a skipped focus session isn't counted). On a computer, Space starts and pauses.
  • It's the same timer and music as the flashcards' dropdown: start it here, go study cards, and it keeps going.

📖 Reading tracker

  • Choose 📖 Reading in the Mode menu. + Add a book: give it a title, an author (optional), and either the number of chapters or their names, one per line.
  • Don't know the chapters? Save the book with just its title. It shows "more to come" with a striped bar. Each time you finish a chapter (with +1 chapter, or after a reading session) Qarbon asks its name and has a That was the final chapter box; tick it on the last one and the book is finished. You can also tick More chapters to come in the form if you only know some of them.
  • Each book shows a progress bar, how many chapters are done, the next chapter, and your reading time. +1 chapter and − adjust it; ✎ edits or deletes the book.
  • Search books: type a title or author in the box at the top and press Search. Tap a match and its title and author fill in, and its year, cover and page count are kept. If one of its editions on Open Library lists the chapters (or it's in Qarbon's library), the chapter names fill in too; otherwise, type the number of chapters. Searching needs the internet; tracking doesn't.
  • From the library: ready-made books (like Siddhartha by Hermann Hesse, with its real chapter names) can be added with one tap. Public-domain books have a link to read them free on Project Gutenberg.
  • 📖 Open book reads a book right in Qarbon. Siddhartha comes with its full text (a public-domain translation): one chapter at a time in a book-style page, with A− / A+ for the text size and ◀ ▶ for chapters. Finish chapter ✓ at the bottom moves your progress bar and opens the next chapter. Qarbon remembers where you stopped (it syncs), and time spent reading counts toward the book.
  • Your own PDFs: in ✎ (edit a book), choose PDF of this book. Then 📄 Open PDF shows it page by page, with A− / A+ to zoom and the same Finish chapter ✓. The PDF stays on that device (it's too big to sync), but your page syncs. The PDF viewer needs the internet the first time.
  • ⏱ Read starts a reading session with the timer: ⏱ Focus opens with a "Reading" bar, and your focus time counts toward that book.
  • After each focus session, or when you tap Done reading, it asks how far you got, and the progress bar moves. Finishing the last chapter moves the book to the Finished shelf.

🎧 Media (the Qpod)

  • Choose 🎧 Media in the Mode menu: the Qpod, Qarbon's take on the 2005 iPod Video, that plays your own songs and YouTube / SoundCloud links. It's the same music as everywhere else in Qarbon, so switching modes never restarts it.
  • Click wheel: circle your thumb around it to scroll; press the center to choose, MENU to go back, ⏮ ⏭ for the song before / after (⏮ first starts the song over), ▶❚❚ to play or pause. You can also tap a line on the screen.
  • Music sorts your songs into Playlists (Recently Added, Played This Session), Artists, Albums and Songs, using the names inside the files. Playing from an album or artist keeps ⏮ ⏭ in it ("3 of 12"). Shuffle Songs plays everything in a random order.
  • Now Playing: on iPhone, turning the wheel moves through the song (web pages can't change an iPhone's volume). You can also tap or drag the progress bar to jump to any spot. On a Mac it changes the volume; press the center to move through the song instead.
  • Visualizer (your own songs): on Now Playing press the center (on a Mac press it twice), or tap the small round button in the Qpod's bottom-left corner. Three looks: Waveform, the whole song as bars that fill in purple as it plays (tap or drag along it to jump); iTunes, rainbow bars and a glowing wave moving with the music; and Space, a slow flight through stars and real Hubble nebulas that glows a little with the bass. Press the center to go to the next look (or Settings › Visualizer), turn the wheel to move through the song, MENU to go back. The first time, a song takes a few seconds to read ("working…"); after that it opens straight away. YouTube and SoundCloud don't let Qarbon read their sound, so it only works with songs from your phone. It never touches the music itself, so your songs keep playing with the phone locked.
  • Videos: your YouTube and SoundCloud links (and Add a Link… at the end). YouTube plays right in the Qpod's screen; SoundCloud's player is too wide for the small iPod, so it plays in the music panel under it (in full screen it fits in the screen).
  • Add Music (in the Qpod): Add a Link… (paste or type a YouTube / SoundCloud link, then Play), Add Files… (songs from your phone) and On This Phone (your songs: choose one to play it or remove it from the phone; removing asks first).
  • Full screen: tap the small round button in the Qpod's bottom corner and the Qpod fills the whole screen, with everything for listening inside it. Tap it again, or hold MENU, to come back. The music keeps playing either way. On a Mac: F switches it, Esc on the main menu leaves.
  • Now Playing is always at the top of the Qpod's menu.
  • Settings (in the Qpod): Shuffle, Repeat, Clicker (the tick sound), Color, Full Screen, Visualizer (Waveform, iTunes or Space), Remove Music, and About (your songs, videos, the version, and a note on playing with the iPhone locked).
  • Under the Qpod: the Color ▾ dropdown (Qarbon Fiber, the default: carbon with a machined wheel, White, Silver, Red, Blue, Green, U2, Neon, or Clear: fully see-through, so it melts into space), then the whole music panel (paste a link, add files, My music, Played this session, Remove music), then the timer dropdown (just the timer here).
  • On a Mac: ↑ ↓ scroll, Enter chooses, Esc goes back, Space plays or pauses, ← → skip.

Timer and music

  • Tap the time at the top to open the small timer, or choose ⏱ Focus in the Mode menu for the big one.
  • Styles: Pomodoro 25/5 (a 15 min break every 4th time), Long Pomodoro 50/10 (a 30 min break every 3rd time), 52/17, Deep work 90/20, 15 min, 30 min, 1 hour, or Custom.
  • Custom (set in ⏱ Focus): choose the focus, break and long-break lengths, and after how many focus sessions the long break comes.
  • A chime plays when time is up, and the next part waits for you to press Start. Finished focus sessions are counted.
  • Music: paste a YouTube or SoundCloud link and press Play. It keeps playing when you close the panel or switch pages (a ♪ shows next to the time). Remove music clears it.
  • Your own songs: 📁 Choose music files opens the Files app (On My iPhone, iCloud Drive, Downloads). Pick one or several: MP3, M4A, AAC and WAV work best. Their name, artist and cover come from the file. They're kept on this device (not synced, too big) under My music on this phone, where ✕ removes one. Songs from Apple Music can't be used: Apple doesn't let websites read them.
  • Your own songs play in Qarbon's player: a progress bar you can drag, ⏮ ❚❚ ⏭, 🔀 shuffle and 🔁 repeat (tap again for 🔂, repeat one song). Their name and buttons also show on the lock screen.
  • 🕘 Played this session (a dropdown, just above Remove music): the last 10 songs, newest first. Tap one to play it again. It's forgotten when you close Qarbon.
  • ⏮ Previous / Next ⏭ move through a playlist (YouTube playlists, and SoundCloud links with /sets/ in them), through your own songs, or else through Played this session.
  • On iPhone: YouTube and SoundCloud pause when the screen locks or you switch apps (Apple's and YouTube's rules; sometimes ▶ in Control Center starts them again). Your own songs usually keep going. Added to the Home Screen, music may stop sooner. Inside Qarbon, switching modes never stops the music.
  • YouTube may not play when index.html is opened straight from the Mac. It works on the website.

Voice

  • Choose Female (Amélie) or Male (Thomas) in ⚙ settings. Qarbon uses the French voices installed on your device; if that one isn't there, it uses another French voice.

☁ Account and sync

  • In ⚙ settings, Create account (an email and a password of 6 or more characters) on your first device, then Sign in with the same email and password on your others.
  • Your cards, decks, books, reading time and settings then sync between your devices: when the app opens, a few seconds after you change something, and when you're back online. Sync now does it right away.
  • Each card, deck and book keeps the newest version from either device, so reviewing on your phone and adding a book on your Mac keeps both. Deleting something on one device deletes it on the others.
  • Offline? Everything still works and is saved on the device; it syncs when you're back online. The status line at the bottom of the study page shows when it last synced.
  • Sign out keeps your progress on that device. Your saved progress is private: only your account can read it.

Saving and backups

  • Progress saves automatically, in this browser on this device. Your phone and your Mac keep separate progress.
  • In ⚙ settings: New cards per day sets how many new words Review adds each day.
  • Export backup (in ⚙) downloads a file with all your cards, progress, decks and settings. Restore backup replaces your progress with a backup (it asks first). Use these to move progress between devices.
  • Private browsing windows can block saving. Qarbon shows a warning if that happens.

Space images

  • Sky: the real night sky around the north galactic pole (the darkest part of the sky, away from the Milky Way), drawn from the Yale Bright Star Catalog: 6,331 stars at their true positions, brightness and colors.
  • Black hole: Sagittarius A* in polarized light (2024), EHT Collaboration / ESO, CC BY 4.0.
  • Earth: NASA Earth Observatory: Blue Marble (land and sea), cloud cover, and Black Marble 2016 (city lights).
  • Moon: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio (full Moon, from Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter data). The phase is worked out from the date (Meeus' formula).
  • Big Dipper: the real positions, brightness and colors of its stars, plus Alcor.
  • Sun: NASA's Solar Dynamics Observatory (in extreme ultraviolet light, 304 Å, October 2026).
  • Crab Nebula (Hubble's mosaic, 2005) and Helix Nebula (Hubble): NASA, ESA, STScI.

iPhone tips

  • In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen to open Qarbon like an app.
  • Keep Safari's zoom at 100%.
  • Sound only starts after a tap (Apple's rule). YouTube and SoundCloud stop when the screen locks; your own music files usually keep playing.
  • The build number at the bottom of the study page tells you which version you have.

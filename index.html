How to use Qarbon

Getting started

  • The home screen shows one world at a time, starting with the black hole. It moves on to the next world by itself every 5 seconds, like a little tour. Touching it pauses the tour; leave it alone for 10 seconds and it carries on (not with Reduce Motion on). Swipe left or right to move between them yourself (on a computer: the ‹ › buttons or the arrow keys). Review is the Earth, Decks the Big Dipper, Translate the Tower of Babel, Practice the Moon, B.A.R. Exam the Sun (tap it and watch), Focus the Crab Nebula, Qarbon Media Player the Qarbon atom wearing headphones, and Reading the Helix Nebula. Tap a world to go straight there. The black hole opens Review on your last deck. The small line under each world shows cards due, your deck, tonight's Moon, or the books you're reading.
  • They're all real: the black hole is Sagittarius A*, the one at the center of our galaxy, as photographed by the Event Horizon Telescope. The Earth is live: it turns, and it's lit where the Sun really is right now, with city lights on the night side. The Moon shows tonight's actual phase, and the stars behind everything are the real night sky (see Space images below).
  • The app opens on the deck you used last (Level 1 the first time).
  • Pick what you're doing from the Mode menu at the top: 🃏 Review, 🗂 Decks, 🔁 Practice, 🔥 B.A.R. Exam, ⏱ Focus, 📖 Reading, 🌐 Translate or 🎧 Qarbon Media Player.
  • Built-in stacks: Level 1 (the 150 most useful words), Level 2 (150 more) and Level 3 (300 words for everyday life: home, city, travel, work, health, feelings; levels A2–B1), then Level 4 (B2), Level 5 (C1) and Level 6 (C2), 75 harder words each, where the rare cards live. All my cards has everything.
  • Switch decks in 🗂 Decks mode: tap Switch ▾ at the top and pick one. The deck you're in is highlighted.
  • Make your own deck: in Decks → Switch ▾, tap + New deck from a CSV file, pick the file and name it. It stays saved. A word that's in two decks shares one set of progress.
  • Your own decks have ✎ to rename them and 🗑 to delete them. Deleting a deck keeps its cards in All my cards.
  • CSV format: the first row needs front (French) and back (English). Optional columns: part_of_speech, gender (m, f or m/f), example_target, example_english, tags (levels like A1, A2, B1, B2, separated by ;).
  • On the study page, ‹ (top left) or the Qarbon logo goes back to the black hole, ⚙ opens the settings, and ? opens this page.

Review

  • Shows the cards due today, plus the new words from the packs you've opened.
  • Today's pack: new words come in a pack of 10 each day. Tap "Today's pack is ready" above the card, tap the pack to tear it open, then tap to turn over each card. The hardest world comes last, face down. About 1 daily pack in 10 has a rare pull: a Nebula or Black Hole word from Level 6.
  • More packs: when you're done for the day you can open another pack. Each one adds to your reviews over the next days. Its size is Cards per extra pack in ⚙.
  • Flip: tap the card, tap Show answer, or press Space/Enter. Tap again to peek back at the front.
  • Rate it: Again, Hard, Good or Easy (keys 1 to 4). The small text under each button is when you'll see the card next.
  • Swipe right for Good, left for Again (after flipping).
  • Again brings the card back a few cards later today, and again tomorrow.
  • Scheduling works like Anki: every card has an "ease" (starts at 2.5). Good multiplies the wait by it. Hard grows the wait a little and lowers the ease. Easy jumps further and raises it. Again resets the card to 1 day. Words you find hard come back more often.
  • 🔊 says the word or the example sentence. The color band along the top of the card (and the word-type label) shows the word type: noun green, verb pink, adjective orange, adverb purple, pronoun blue.
  • Every word is a world. The cards are collectibles on a trip out from Earth: each level has two worlds, its easier half and its harder half. Level A1 is Earth, then the Moon; A2 is Venus, then Mars; B1 is Mercury, then Jupiter; B2 (Level 4) is Saturn, then Uranus; C1 (Level 5) is Neptune, then the Sun; and the rarest cards, C2 (Level 6), are Nebulas and Black Holes. The world's real photo is behind the word, and its name is at the top right. "Harder" is worked out from the word itself: its level, accents, and length (or several words).
  • Mastery: the four little bars at the bottom show how well you know a word: New, Learning (after your first right answer), Familiar, Known and Mastered, from how long until the card's next review. Easy worlds master fast (one Easy on a new Earth word masters it); Black Holes take 60 days. As a word moves up, its holo foil gets stronger, the panel's edge turns silver, then gold, and Mastered cards sparkle. A gold "⬆" note says when an answer moved a word up.
  • The shine: drag across a card (or move the mouse over it) and the light slides across its silver edge and foil. On a phone you can let it follow the tilt instead: ⚙ → Card shine follows tilt (iPhone asks permission once). Swiping still grades the card.
  • Reverse cards: in ⚙ → Card direction, each stack can be French → English, English → French (you answer in French; the card shows EN → FR), or Both. Each direction has its own schedule. With Both, a new word's English → French side unlocks once you've seen it French-first.

Decks

  • Browse every card in the current deck: search, filter by level or word type, and see each card's status (new, due today, or its next date).
  • Practice these practices just the cards you filtered.
  • Tap a card to edit it. Change the spelling, meaning, word type, gender, example or tags; its progress stays.
  • Suspend a card to stop it coming up in Review, Practice and Burn (it shows as "suspended" in the list). Unsuspend it any time.
  • Delete removes a card and its progress. Words from the built-in stacks (Level 1, Level 2, Level 3) can be suspended but not deleted.
  • + Add card makes a new card. In your own deck it goes into that deck; otherwise it goes into All my cards.

🪐 Solar System

  • Your French as a trip out from Earth to the Black Hole: two worlds per level (Level 1 = Earth and the Moon, Level 2 = Venus and Mars … Level 6 = a Nebula and the Black Hole). Swipe the map sideways, tap a world for its progress.
  • Reaching a world: 80% of its cards at Familiar or better (you've remembered them across a few days). Then the next world opens.
  • Packs follow the map: they only deal cards from worlds that are open, so a deck further out waits until you get there. Cards already in your binder can always be reviewed, and your own decks are never locked.

🌐 Translate

  • Choose 🌐 Translate in the Mode menu and type a word or a short phrase. It looks it up after a short pause (or press Enter). ⇄ switches between French → English and English → French.
  • The answer shows as the card you'd get: colored by word type, with the gender, le / la for nouns, and an example sentence when there is one. Tap the French or the English to change it, and 🔊 to hear it.
  • Add to: pick one of your own decks (or + New deck…), then + Add card. Without a deck it goes into All my cards. The built-in decks are fixed word lists, so cards can't be added to them.
  • Other meanings: tap one to put it on the card instead.
  • Where the answers come from: your own cards and the built-in decks first (instant, and they work offline), then Wiktionary for single words (word type, gender, examples), and MyMemory for phrases. Both are free with no account. MyMemory allows about 5,000 characters a day; after that it says so, and works again the next day.
  • Offline, or the services are down? Words from your decks still show. For anything else, Qarbon says so and leaves the card open, so you can type the translation yourself and still add it.

Practice

  • Goes through every card in the stack in random order. It never changes your schedule, so practice as much as you like.
  • Cards you miss come back at the end, and you get a score when you finish.

🔥 B.A.R. Exam (Burn After Reading)

  • Choose 🔥 B.A.R. Exam in the Mode menu. It takes 10 cards you've never studied from the stack (fewer if that's all that's left).
  • Read each one once, and hear it. There's no going back.
  • The cards burn up. Then for each English word, pick the exact French spelling out of 4: the right one, an accent trap, a spelling trap, and another real word.
  • Right counts as Good, wrong as Hard, and missed words go first in your next Review. The end screen shows how many "survived the burn".

⏱ Focus (timer and music for any studying)

  • Choose ⏱ Focus in the Mode menu (or ⤢ Focus page in the small timer dropdown). The Mode menu takes you straight back to Review or anything else.
  • At the top is the ♪ music dropdown: closed, it shows what's playing; tap it for the player and your lists. Then a big clock with a ring that empties as time passes. Start/Pause, Reset, and Skip (jump to the break, or back to focus; a skipped focus session isn't counted). On a computer, Space starts and pauses.
  • It's the same timer and music as the flashcards' dropdown: start it here, go study cards, and it keeps going.

📖 Reading tracker

  • Choose 📖 Reading in the Mode menu. + Add a book: give it a title, an author (optional), and either the number of chapters or their names, one per line.
  • Don't know the chapters? Save the book with just its title. It shows "more to come" with a striped bar. Each time you finish a chapter (with +1 chapter, or after a reading session) Qarbon asks its name and has a That was the final chapter box; tick it on the last one and the book is finished. You can also tick More chapters to come in the form if you only know some of them.
  • Each book shows a progress bar, how many chapters are done, the next chapter, and your reading time. +1 chapter and − adjust it; ✎ edits or deletes the book.
  • Search any book: type a title or author in the box at the top. Results come as you type (from 3 letters), from two free catalogs at once, Open Library and Google Books, with each book shown once. Books you can read inside Qarbon come first, under Read in Qarbon. About 20 show at a time; scroll to the end for 20 more. Tap a match and its title and author fill in, and its year, cover and page count are kept. If one of its editions on Open Library lists the chapters, the chapter names fill in too; otherwise, type the number of chapters. Searching needs the internet (offline, the Read in Qarbon books still show); tracking doesn't. Google sometimes turns away searches without an account when it's busy; then the results are Open Library's only.
  • Read in Qarbon: 13 free books you can read right in the app, shown as covers (tap Show all for every one): Siddhartha, Demian and Steppenwolf (Hesse), The Metamorphosis (Kafka), Notes from Underground (Dostoevsky), Candide (Voltaire), The Prophet (Gibran), Flatland (Abbott), Heart of Darkness (Conrad), Bartleby, the Scrivener (Melville), Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde (Stevenson), The Time Machine (Wells) and The Sorrows of Young Werther (Goethe). Tap a cover to start reading: it's added to your books with its real chapter names (✓ In my books). All are public domain in the US; their texts come from Project Gutenberg (header and license left out), and the covers from Open Library. Each book's text only loads when you open it, so the app stays light.
  • 📖 Open book reads a book right in Qarbon: one chapter at a time in a book-style page, with A− / A+ for the text size and ◀ ▶ for chapters. Finish chapter ✓ at the bottom moves your progress bar and opens the next chapter. Qarbon remembers where you stopped, even if you leave the app right away, and it syncs: if you read on another device, a book you left open here jumps to where you got to. Time spent reading counts toward the book.
  • Your own PDFs: in ✎ (edit a book), choose PDF of this book. Then 📄 Open PDF shows it page by page, with A− / A+ to zoom and the same Finish chapter ✓. The PDF stays on that device (it's too big to sync), but your page syncs. The PDF viewer needs the internet the first time.
  • ⏱ Read starts a reading session with the timer: ⏱ Focus opens with a "Reading" bar, and your focus time counts toward that book.
  • After each focus session, or when you tap Done reading, it asks how far you got, and the progress bar moves. Finishing the last chapter moves the book to the Finished shelf.

🎧 Qarbon Media Player (the Qpod)

  • Choose 🎧 Qarbon Media Player in the Mode menu: the Qpod, Qarbon's take on the 2005 iPod Video, that plays your own songs and YouTube / SoundCloud links. It's the same music as everywhere else in Qarbon, so switching modes never restarts it.
  • Click wheel: circle your thumb around it to scroll; press the center to choose, MENU to go back, ⏮ ⏭ for the song before / after (⏮ first starts the song over), ▶❚❚ to play or pause. You can also tap a line on the screen.
  • Your playlists mix your own songs with YouTube and SoundCloud links. Make one in Music › Playlists › New Playlist…, then fill it with Add Songs & Videos (✓ = it's in). A song's menu (On This Phone › the song) has Add to Playlist too. Choose a playlist to play it from any item, or Shuffle; ⏮ ⏭ and "3 of 12" stay inside it, and when a song ends the next item plays, links included. If the phone won't start a link by itself (for example while it's locked), the link waits on Now Playing: press ▶. Edit Playlist: Add Songs & Videos, Move Songs (press the center to pick one up, turn the wheel to move it, press again to put it down), Remove Songs, Rename…, Delete Playlist. Playlists sync between your devices. Your song files don't (they're too big), so on your other device a song that isn't there shows as "not on this phone" and is skipped; add the same file there and it's found by its name, artist and album.
  • Music sorts your songs into Playlists (Recently Added, Played This Session, then yours), Artists, Albums and Songs, using the names inside the files. Playing from an album or artist keeps ⏮ ⏭ in it ("3 of 12"). Shuffle Songs plays everything in a random order.
  • Now Playing: on iPhone, turning the wheel moves through the song (web pages can't change an iPhone's volume). You can also tap or drag the progress bar to jump to any spot. On a Mac it changes the volume; press the center to move through the song instead.
  • Visualizer: on Now Playing press the center (on a Mac press it twice), or tap the small round button in the Qpod's bottom-left corner. Four looks: Waveform, the whole song as bars that fill in purple as it plays (tap or drag along it to jump); iTunes, rainbow bars and a glowing wave moving with the music; and Space, a slow flight through stars and real Hubble nebulas that glows a little with the bass; and Cover, the song's picture filling the screen, drifting slowly while it plays, with a thin line showing how far in you are (no picture, or YouTube / SoundCloud: the Qarbon atom). In Space and Cover, tap the screen to fill the whole phone; tap again (Esc on a Mac) to come back. Press the center to go to the next look (or Settings › Visualizer), turn the wheel to move through the song, MENU to go back. The first time, a song takes a few seconds to read ("working…"); after that it opens straight away. YouTube and SoundCloud don't let Qarbon read their sound, so the first three looks only work with songs from your phone (Cover works with anything). It never touches the music itself, so your songs keep playing with the phone locked.
  • Videos: your YouTube and SoundCloud links (and Add a Link… at the end). YouTube plays right in the Qpod's screen; SoundCloud's player is too wide for the small iPod, so it plays in the music panel under it (in full screen it fits in the screen).
  • Add Music (in the Qpod): Add a Link… (paste or type a YouTube / SoundCloud link, then Play), Add Files… (songs from your phone) and On This Phone (your songs: choose one to play it, give it Album Art, or remove it from the phone; removing asks first).
  • Album Art: pick any picture from your photos for a song, or for its Whole Album at once. It replaces the cover from the file on Now Playing, in your song list and on the lock screen. Original Art goes back to the file's own cover (Remove Picture if it had none). Big photos are made small first, and the pictures stay on this device with the songs.
  • Full screen: tap the small round button in the Qpod's bottom corner and the Qpod fills the whole screen, with everything for listening inside it. Tap it again, or hold MENU, to come back. The music keeps playing either way. On a Mac: F switches it, Esc on the main menu leaves.
  • Now Playing is always at the top of the Qpod's menu.
  • Mini Qpod: while music is loaded, a small see-through Qpod floats at the side of the screen in the other modes (Review, Reading…). Tap it to go back to the Qpod; when you're done, ‹ (or MENU on the Qpod's main menu) takes you back to where you were, on the same card or page. Drag it up or down, or to the other side, and it stays there. Swipe it off the side to put it away: the music keeps playing (pause or stop it from the timer's music panel), and it comes back next time you open the Qpod.
  • Settings (in the Qpod): Shuffle, Repeat, Clicker (the tick sound), Color, Full Screen, Visualizer (Waveform, iTunes, Space or Cover), Remove Music, and About (your songs, videos, the version, and a note on playing with the iPhone locked).
  • Under the Qpod: the Color ▾ dropdown (Qarbon Fiber, the default: carbon with a machined wheel, White, Silver, Red, Blue, Green, U2, Neon, or Clear: fully see-through, so it melts into space), then the whole music panel (paste a link, add files, 🎶 Playlists, My music, Played this session, Remove music). In 🎶 Playlists (in every music panel), tap a playlist to play it, or ⋯ for Play, Shuffle, Rename and Delete; its picture is its first song's. Add to a playlist with ⋯ › ＋ Add to playlist in My music, or the ＋ next to a row in Played this session, then the timer dropdown (just the timer here).
  • On a Mac: ↑ ↓ scroll, Enter chooses, Esc goes back, Space plays or pauses, ← → skip.

Timer and music

  • Tap the time at the top to open the small timer, or choose ⏱ Focus in the Mode menu for the big one.
  • Styles: Pomodoro 25/5 (a 15 min break every 4th time), Long Pomodoro 50/10 (a 30 min break every 3rd time), 52/17, Deep work 90/20, 15 min, 30 min, 1 hour, or Custom.
  • Custom (set in ⏱ Focus): choose the focus, break and long-break lengths, and after how many focus sessions the long break comes.
  • A chime plays when time is up, and the next part waits for you to press Start. Finished focus sessions are counted.
  • Music: paste a YouTube or SoundCloud link and press Play. It keeps playing when you close the panel or switch pages (a ♪ shows next to the time). Remove music clears it.
  • Your own songs: 📁 Choose music files opens the Files app (On My iPhone, iCloud Drive, Downloads). Pick one or several: MP3, M4A, AAC and WAV work best. Their name, artist and cover come from the file. They're kept on this device (not synced, too big) under My music on this phone, each with its cover. ⋯ next to a song: 🖼 picks album art for it (or its whole album), ↺ goes back to the original, ✕ Remove song removes it. Songs from Apple Music can't be used: Apple doesn't let websites read them.
  • Your own songs play in Qarbon's player: a progress bar you can drag, ⏮ ❚❚ ⏭, 🔀 shuffle and 🔁 repeat (tap again for 🔂, repeat one song). Their name and buttons also show on the lock screen.
  • 🕘 Played this session (a dropdown, just above Remove music): the last 10 songs, newest first. Tap one to play it again. It's forgotten when you close Qarbon.
  • ⏮ Previous / Next ⏭ move through a playlist (YouTube playlists, and SoundCloud links with /sets/ in them), through your own songs, or else through Played this session.
  • On iPhone: YouTube and SoundCloud pause when the screen locks or you switch apps (Apple's and YouTube's rules; sometimes ▶ in Control Center starts them again). Your own songs usually keep going. Added to the Home Screen, music may stop sooner. Inside Qarbon, switching modes never stops the music.
  • YouTube with the phone locked (tip): play the video full screen (YouTube's ⛶ button), lock the phone, then press ▶ on the lock screen. iOS then plays it like any video, so it keeps going, and the screen being off saves battery. Apple may change this in an iOS update.
  • YouTube may not play when index.html is opened straight from the Mac. It works on the website.

Voice

  • Choose Female (Amélie) or Male (Thomas) in ⚙ settings. Qarbon uses the French voices installed on your device; if that one isn't there, it uses another French voice.

☁ Account and sync

  • In ⚙ settings, Create account (an email and a password of 6 or more characters) on your first device, then Sign in with the same email and password on your others.
  • Your cards, decks, books, reading time and settings then sync between your devices: when the app opens, a few seconds after you change something, and when you're back online. Sync now does it right away.
  • Each card, deck and book keeps the newest version from either device, so reviewing on your phone and adding a book on your Mac keeps both. Deleting something on one device deletes it on the others.
  • Offline? Everything still works and is saved on the device; it syncs when you're back online. The status line at the bottom of the study page shows when it last synced.
  • Sign out keeps your progress on that device. Your saved progress is private: only your account can read it.

Saving and backups

  • Progress saves automatically, in this browser on this device. Your phone and your Mac keep separate progress.
  • In ⚙ settings: Cards per extra pack sets how many new words an extra pack has (today's pack is always 10).
  • Export backup (in ⚙) downloads a file with all your cards, progress, decks and settings. Restore backup replaces your progress with a backup (it asks first). Use these to move progress between devices.
  • Private browsing windows can block saving. Qarbon shows a warning if that happens.

Space images

  • Sky: the real night sky around the north galactic pole (the darkest part of the sky, away from the Milky Way), drawn from the Yale Bright Star Catalog: 6,331 stars at their true positions, brightness and colors.
  • Black hole: Sagittarius A* in polarized light (2024), EHT Collaboration / ESO, CC BY 4.0.
  • Earth: NASA Earth Observatory: Blue Marble (land and sea), cloud cover, and Black Marble 2016 (city lights).
  • Moon: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio (full Moon, from Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter data). The phase is worked out from the date (Meeus' formula).
  • Big Dipper: the real positions, brightness and colors of its stars, plus Alcor.
  • Sun: NASA's Solar Dynamics Observatory (in extreme ultraviolet light, 304 Å, October 2026).
  • Crab Nebula (Hubble's mosaic, 2005) and Helix Nebula (Hubble): NASA, ESA, STScI.
  • Card worlds (all public domain, via Wikimedia Commons): Mercury NASA / Johns Hopkins APL / Carnegie Institution (MESSENGER); Venus NASA (Mariner 10); Mars NASA / USGS (Viking); Jupiter NASA, ESA, A. Simon (Hubble, 2014); Saturn NASA / JPL / Space Science Institute (Cassini, 2008); Uranus NASA / JPL (Voyager 2); Neptune NASA / JPL (Voyager 2, processed by Aster Cowart). Earth, the Moon, the Sun, the Crab Nebula and Sagittarius A* are the photos above.
  • Tower of Babel (not from space, but it's where the story says languages came from): Pieter Bruegel the Elder's painting, about 1563, Museum Boijmans Van Beuningen, Rotterdam. Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons (Google Art Project).

iPhone tips

  • In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen to open Qarbon like an app.
  • Keep Safari's zoom at 100%.
  • Sound only starts after a tap (Apple's rule). YouTube and SoundCloud stop when the screen locks; your own music files usually keep playing.
  • The build number at the bottom of the study page tells you which version you have.

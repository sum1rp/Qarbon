Privacy and terms

Last updated October 28, 2026 · Qarbon is a free study app made by one person, under Unknown x Unknown.

The short version: Qarbon keeps your study progress so it can follow you between your devices. It doesn't show ads, doesn't sell or share your data, and doesn't track you with analytics. You can delete your account at any time.

Using Qarbon without an account

Everything you do (your cards, progress, books, study time, scores and settings) is saved only in your browser on that device. Nothing is sent to us.

What an account keeps online

  • Your email and password. The password is stored scrambled (hashed) by our sign-in service; nobody can read it, including us.
  • Your username (like @jazz). Other signed-in people can see it, for example to find you as a friend or on a leaderboard later.
  • Your save: your cards and their progress, your own decks, playlists (their names and links), books and reading places, study time and streaks, game scores, and your settings. It's private: only your account can read it.

Some things never leave your device, account or not: music files and their pictures, PDFs you attach to books, and the book you're reading at the moment.

Who helps run Qarbon

  • Supabase stores accounts and saves (the database and sign-in).
  • Resend sends the emails with your sign-up and password codes.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.

Some features ask other free services for things while you use them: book search (Open Library, Google Books), word lookups in Translate (Wiktionary, MyMemory), the pixel fonts in the Arcade (Google Fonts), the PDF viewer (cdnjs) and music links you paste (YouTube, SoundCloud). Like any website, they see your device's internet address when they answer. Qarbon only sends them what's needed, like the word or book title you searched for.

Deleting your data

In Qarbon, open ⚙ → Account settings → Delete my account. Your account, username and online save are removed for good. Your device keeps its own copy of your progress until you clear it (or the browser does).

Children

Qarbon accounts are for people 13 and older.

Terms of use

  • Qarbon is free and provided as it is. It may change, have bugs, or be down sometimes. Keep a backup (⚙ → Export backup) of anything you can't lose.
  • Your cards, notes and progress are yours.
  • Usernames must not pretend to be someone else, or be hateful or offensive. Accounts that break this, or misuse Qarbon (like trying to get into other people's data), can be removed.
  • The books in “Read in Qarbon” are public domain texts from Project Gutenberg. The space pictures are from NASA and other public sources (see the how-to page).
  • These terms may be updated. Big changes will be shown in the app.
